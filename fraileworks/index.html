You might be wondering

A few good
questions.

Do I need to know anything about AI?

No. Tell me what happens in your business today and what you'd like to make easier. I'll explain the options in plain language.

What if I don't actually need AI?

Then we use something simpler. A good website, a form, or a straightforward automation may be all you need.

Can you work with the tools I already use?

That's where we start. I'll check what your current tools allow before promising an integration or asking you to move anything.

Am I tied to you after the project?

You own the code and accounts. Support is optional, and you can have another developer work on the project later.

What if the project changes?

We agree on the scope up front. If you want to add something, we discuss the extra work, price, and timing before I start it.
