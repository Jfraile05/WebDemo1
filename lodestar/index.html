Process

How a project runs

Small enough to move fast, structured enough that you know what you are getting before anything is built.

Step 01

Scope call

Thirty minutes on what the problem costs you today. You leave with a written scope, a price, and a timeline, or with an honest no if AI is the wrong tool.

Step 02

Build

You see working software early and often, not a slide deck. Most projects ship in two to six weeks depending on how many systems have to be touched.

Step 03

Handover

It runs on your accounts, under your domain, with documentation your team can follow. Support after launch is available, never assumed.
